License reports and notices

The image build produces two complementary outputs: a license scan for review and an attribution attachment for the published image.

License reports

Before publication, Trivy scans image.tar using supported package metadata and recognizable license text. The JSON and text reports preserve all severity levels, including UNKNOWN. Counts in the job summary help reviewers locate findings; they do not determine whether a dependency is acceptable for a particular use.

The pipeline is report-only for license findings. This allows evidence to be reviewed before a blocking policy is established, while scanner or formatting errors still fail the job.

Third-party notices

After publication, the collector runs against the exact pushed digest. It gathers installed RPM packages, including base-image packages, Python distributions, and Helm plugin license and attribution files. Known downloaded tools are listed even when notice files are unavailable.

ORAS attaches the resulting NOTICE together with the repository's LICENSE as an OCI artifact associated with the image digest. Keeping these as an attachment lets consumers retrieve attribution for that image without adding generated files to the repository or placing them inside the image.

Coverage and interpretation

Notice collection is best-effort. Missing notices and embedded or unmanaged dependencies remain items for review. A scan cannot establish complete dependency coverage, license compatibility, or fulfillment of source-distribution and attribution obligations. Neither a successful scan nor a generated notice is legal clearance.

The retrieval guide explains where to find both outputs. The CI reference records the report settings.
